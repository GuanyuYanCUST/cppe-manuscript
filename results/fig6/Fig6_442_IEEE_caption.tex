  \caption{Security and conditional utility for frozen legitimate-arm replay
    ($k_{\mathrm{req}}=10$; five models, four domains).
    (a) Per-domain, per-model unauthorized effects as observed by deterministic
    in-harness PEP/tool-sandbox probes. Each cell has 200 matched histories;
    B1 whiskers are nominal 95\% Wilson intervals and PG open circles denote
    observed zero counts. Domain-specific axis limits are printed in each title.
    (b) PG proposal dispositions by domain and model, plotted as mutually
    exclusive 100\% stacked bars. The denominator for each bar is that
    model-domain cell's proposed-rule count, printed immediately above the bar;
    domain totals are printed in each panel title.
    (c) PG installed rules and canonical valid PEP hits divided by the model's
    proposed-rule count, with nominal 95\% Wilson whiskers; canonical hits are
    \emph{not} end-to-end agent task success.
    PG's class-scoped root capability is automatically issued by the
    experimental harness from a fixed template rather than granted by a
    real-world administrator.
    (d) Issuer-unsafe installations, out-of-root installations, and unauthorized
    sandbox effects, each per 4,000 matched legitimate histories. B3 is a
    containment-only judge without a typed root grant. The 598 B1 out-of-root
    installations include 584 sandbox-probe-visible effects. Empty markers are
    observed zeros rather than a guarantee of zero population risk
    (PG: 0/4,000 sandbox effects; two-sided 95\% exact upper bound 0.0922\%).
    Samples share frozen histories and potentially correlated channel/seed
    configurations; nominal intervals do not establish cross-channel
    independence.}
  \label{fig:pg-security-utility}
